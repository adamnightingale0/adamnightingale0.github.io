How I teach

I believe learning works best when it feels more like a conversation, which is why I use the Socratic method. It’s centred on asking questions and encouraging curiosity — guiding a student step by step so they discover the ideas, patterns and solutions themselves.

My lessons are structured but never rigid. I adapt to each student’s needs, set clear goals, and create a supportive space where it’s okay to make mistakes and learn from them. The aim isn’t just to pass exams, but to build lasting confidence and understanding.
