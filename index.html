About my teaching style

I believe learning works best when it feels more like a conversation, which is why I use the Socratic method of teaching. This technique is centred around asking questions and encouraging curiosity. The idea is to guide the student step by step so they discover ideas, patterns, and solutions on their own.

My lessons are structured but never rigid. I adapt to each student’s needs, set clear goals, and create a supportive space where it’s okay to make mistakes and learn from them. The aim isn’t just to pass exams, but to build lasting confidence and understanding of the subject.

I’m deeply passionate about encouraging engagement and curiosity in science, and I run a blog about Life Science topics at www.newscleus.co.uk.
